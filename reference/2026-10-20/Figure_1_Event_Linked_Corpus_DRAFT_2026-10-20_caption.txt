Figure 1. Event-linked Earth datasets preserve the physical meaning of distinct modalities. A common physical event connects observations, existing simulation archives, impact/exposure information and scientific-agent evaluation tasks. The enhancement records each view's native sampling, spatial and temporal support, measurement or model configuration, transformations, uncertainty, provenance and permitted uses. The project will test whether these explicit relationships improve scientific comparison, transfer and traceable inference. This is an illustrative design, not a measured result, a fixed universal schema or a commitment by an external partner.
