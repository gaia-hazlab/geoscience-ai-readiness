Figure 2. A controlled experiment treats AI readiness as a relationship among a corpus, a learning method and a scientific task. Variants of the same permitted holdings isolate changes in coverage, scale, compression or metadata wherever feasible. Reference models and task definitions are held fixed, and evaluation partitions separate events, regions or time periods before final testing. The team will test whether candidate diagnostics predict downstream performance, transfer, uncertainty, failure and justified abstention. Independent evidence, including negative results, will support conditional readiness profiles and revisions to the corpus. All symbols depict the proposed experiment; no empirical outcomes or universal readiness thresholds are implied.
